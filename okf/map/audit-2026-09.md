# Scope
The live site downloaded on 2026-09-29 (commit `b9c5f9e`): `index.html` with an inline SVG of **971 polygons**
and `colors.json` with **971 colors**. The measurements come from parsing every polygon.

# How the original map worked (reverse-engineered)
* **Geometry**: flat-top hexagons, circumradius 10, columns 15 apart, odd columns shifted down 8.66;
  37 columns × 36 rows, `viewBox="0 0 600.0 666.217782649107"`. All 971 polygons were exact regular hexagons
  on this lattice, with no two on the same cell. The generator keeps this geometry.
* **Center**: `#ffffff` at (300, 324.45).
* **Radius ≈ darkness**: distance from white correlated −0.83 with HSL lightness (linear fit
  `radius ≈ 345 − 290·L`). Black `#000000` sat on the bottom edge (300, 636.22).
* **Angle ≈ hue**: for saturated colors, `angle ≈ 203° − hue` (circular concentration 0.80), i.e. red on the
  left, yellow upper left, green on top, cyan right, blue lower right, magenta at the bottom.
  The generated map keeps this orientation.
* **Shape**: a filled, roughly hexagonal blob with ragged edges; no holes inside.
* There was no generator in the repository and no documentation; the markup suggests an external script plus
  manual edits.

# Errors found
## Map (SVG)
1. **Colors in the wrong hue sector.** 204 of 798 clearly chromatic colors (chroma ≥ 15, not in the center)
   were more than 45° away from their hue direction (median error 22.6°, 90th percentile 67°). The worst cases:
   * A whole **cluster of pale peaches and apricots** (Peach Puff `#ffdab9`, Papaya `#ffc9a9`, Apricot `#fbceb1`,
     Navajo White `#ffdead`, Champagne `#fad6a5`, …) sat *below* white, in the pink/blue half, 100–145° off.
   * **Browns and ochres in the green sector**: Ochre `#cc7722`, Honiggelb `#a98307` and Currygelb `#9d9101` sat at the top
     among the greens.
   * Light pinks (Pink `#ffc0cb`, Baby Pink `#f4c2c2`, Cotton Candy `#ffbcd9`) were about 90° off.
2. **Greys had no place.** Neutral colors (61 with HSL saturation < 0.08) were scattered at random angles,
   for example `#808080` at the top, `#c1c4c5` at the upper right and `#9da1aa` on the right. There was no grey axis.
3. **No room to add colors.** Every interior cell was taken, so a new light color could only go on the dark rim,
   in the wrong place. Adding colors correctly needs a re-layout, which is why the map is now generated.
4. **Hand-pasted polygon.** The last polygon, Bulgarian Rose `#480607`, was formatted differently from the rest:
   one line, self-closing, no `<title>`. It was clearly added by hand.
5. **`<?xml version="1.0" encoding="UTF-8"?>` inside the HTML body**, just before the SVG wrapper. In HTML this
   is not valid; the parser treats it as a bogus comment. It is a leftover from pasting a standalone `.svg` file.
6. **Baked-in names were stale or unused.** `data-name` and `<title>` were overwritten at startup by
   `hydrateMapTooltips()` from `colors.json`, and 38 polygons carried `data-brands`/`data-aliases` attributes
   that no code read.
7. **Redundant markup.** `stroke="#444444" stroke-width="1" stroke-opacity="0.25"` was repeated on all 971
   polygons, instead of once on a group.
8. **No markers.** Nothing delimited the generated block, so it could not be regenerated safely.

## Related app issues found at the same time
* The selected cell's white outline (`.sel`) was half covered by neighbors painted later (SVG has no z-index).
* `<link rel="preload" href="./colors.json" as="fetch">` had no `crossorigin`, so the browser ignored the preload,
  downloaded `colors.json` twice and logged a console warning.
* `titleCase()` lower-cased a word that starts with a bracket, e.g. "(Crayola)" became "(crayola)".
* Search did not ignore accents, so "kobai" could not find "Kōbai-iro".

Data problems (families, German names, meta counts) are listed in [/data/known-issues.md](/data/known-issues.md).

# What was done
All of the above were fixed in the same change that introduced `tools/colorpedia.py`, which now generates
the map by the rules in [/map/layout-rules.md](/map/layout-rules.md). Measured the same way, after adding
645 colors: the white core is centered, greys form one spoke, and the map passes `tools/colorpedia.py check`.
